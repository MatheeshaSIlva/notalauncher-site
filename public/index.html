The toggles are real.

      
A tile in your shade runs the same shell command the system would. Wifi, bluetooth, data, torch, do not disturb, rotation, battery saver, airplane mode.

      
That is what Shizuku is for. The app tells you exactly which commands it runs and why.

    

  


  

    

      

    

    

      
Recents are real.

      
Your recents view gets the live task list and real thumbnails, so a layout can show them as cards, a stack, a row, or anything else you draw.

Themes are tokens.

      
Colours, type, shapes, motion and the glass material are one file that applies to any layout. Fonts come from Google Fonts by name.

      
Glass is one word in the theme. The engine picks the best implementation your phone can do.

Scripts are sandboxed.

      
A layout with no script is pure data and needs no trust. A plugin declares what it needs and you approve it. Nothing from the store ever gets a shell.

Creators get paid.

      
Set a price or give it away. Google Play handles the purchase. Seventy percent of every sale goes to the person who made the layout, paid out weekly.

      
The rate is published once and not touched.

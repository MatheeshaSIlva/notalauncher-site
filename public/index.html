Not a Launcher
android · shizuku · no root · in development

> a canvas your phone runs

Launchers are settings screens. This is a blank canvas. Add a frame, give it a job — home, drawer, shade, recents — and build it. What you draw ships with real function. Layouts are text files. Creators publish to a store and keep most of every sale.

No newsletter. One email when there is something to install, and one when the editor is ready.

  • Blank frames, real roles

    A frame has no identity until you give it one. The role brings its data, its gestures and its system calls.

  • Dotfiles for your phone

    Every layout is a text document. Hand-write it, version it, fork it, or draw it in the desktop editor.

  • Made by people

    Pick a layout from the store and your whole phone becomes it. Creators set the price and keep the majority.
